<objective>
Close the loop on ORDR-10: when a seller advances a sub-order's status, the parent order's customer receives a push notification, and tapping it opens that order's detail screen directly. The Cloud Function trigger itself was created in 06-01; this plan hardens it (strengthens emulator tests) and ships the Android side — service-level foreground routing, channel creation, MainActivity onNewIntent + Compose LaunchedEffect deep-link, manifest singleTop flag, OrderNotificationConstants single-source-of-truth, the SyncBus that 06-02 ViewModels collect from to trigger FCM-driven sync (CONTEXT D4 trigger c), and the manual smoke procedure.

Purpose: Without deep-link wiring, the notification is just a dead glyph in the tray. Without SyncBus, Room never refreshes on FCM receipt and the customer sees stale status until the next screen open. The Android side here makes ORDR-10 user-observable.

Output: SyncBus + SyncEvent contract + Koin binding; MessagingService extended with `order_status` type routing AND SyncBus emit; MainActivity deep-link consumer; manifest launchMode update; OrderNotificationConstants; strengthened onOrderStatusChange tests; MessagingService SyncBus emission test; manual smoke doc.
</objective>
