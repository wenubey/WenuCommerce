<threat_model>
ASVS L1. Phase 7 plan 03 is display-only (no new writes), so the surface is small:

- **T-07-03-01 — Cross-seller / hidden-review disclosure (severity: medium).** The seller reviews view could leak reviews for products the seller does not own, or reviews hidden via isVisible=false. Mitigation: the entry point is reachable only from the seller's OWN product-management list (productId comes from the seller's own product row), and the ViewModel filters `isVisible` before rendering (Task 2). No arbitrary productId entry from outside the seller's products. The authoritative read scoping is the Firestore rules from 07-01 (reviews readable, product rating public) — this plan adds no new read privilege. Blocked at medium.
- **T-07-03-02 — Aggregate spoofing on cards (severity: low).** Cards read product.averageRating/reviewCount. These are server-maintained (07-01) and product docs are seller/server-written; a tampered client can only mislead its own view. No new trust boundary crossed. Accepted (low).
- **T-07-03-03 — Write-control leakage into read-only seller view (severity: low).** Reusing ReviewCard could accidentally expose the Helpful/submit action to sellers. Mitigation: `showHelpful=false` + acceptance criterion greps for zero write controls in SellerProductReviewsScreen. Blocked.

No high-severity threats. Block-on-high satisfied (none present).
</threat_model>
